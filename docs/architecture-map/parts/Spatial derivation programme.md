# Spatial derivation programme

**State:** `STATED · ONE OF SIX BUILT`
**Group:** Space as a working dimension
**Map:** [[Payload OS Architecture]]

> The display was built first and derives nothing. Space has four other jobs — resolver, join key, validity clock, inference engine — and a fifth that decides what to buy.

## What it is

- Six roles for space with their honest state: display (built), identity (partial: refutation yes, confirmation no), join key (partial: key yes, second line no), validity clock (partial: points yes, boundaries no), inference (absent), tasking (partial: instruments priced, no site to point them at).
- Five derivations ranked by what they would be worth, each with why it belongs to this firm rather than a point vendor, exactly what it needs, and the mistake it invites: flow through geometry, change detection as candidate observation, one receipted spatial aggregate, versioned boundaries, movement signatures.
- A capability map naming the module or surface that carries each capability, with no component identifier the repository does not actually use.
- The discipline: buy the commodity, own the provenance. PostGIS, S2, H3 and STAC are somebody else's product; the resolution bound, the refusal and the recorded boundary judgment are the firm's.

## Boundaries

- Nothing here acquires imagery, registers a source or tasks an instrument.
- A globe is not an inference: nothing becomes a fact by being drawn on a map.
- Imagery is where source rights bite hardest; every derivation touching it is gated by the rights index, not by capability.
