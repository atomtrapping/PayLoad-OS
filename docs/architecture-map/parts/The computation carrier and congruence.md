# The computation carrier and congruence

**State:** `DOCTRINE · TWO OF FOUR CARD PROPERTIES · NOTHING EXPORTED`
**Group:** Doctrine and governance (applies to every layer)
**Map:** [[Payload OS Architecture]]

> The punch card, not the proof. Credibility lives in the estate, the rulings and the two clocks; the carrier's only job is to carry that across decades. And a manifest is a perfect mirror of the model, which makes it a perfect mirror of the model's errors.

## What it is

- **The carrier reading, chosen over the proof reading.** General zero-knowledge proving of corpus computation is refused with reasons: proving cost, a new trust surface for the reference channel to guard, and a question this audience is not asking — because for a professional performing due diligence, re-derivation beats verification.
- **Four card properties**, two present (determinism under serialisation, auditable without re-execution) and two absent (archivability past the runtime, one shape for every computation). The two absent are the two that make a card a card.
- **The archival test** — readable in thirty years from the specification alone — grading fixed-point as passing, floating-point as degrading, and learned-model inference as failing.
- **Congruence** as the name for three mechanisms already here: the innovation gate for dynamics, the reference channel for frames, the disagreement layer for sources. The frozen trace is the side that never jitters, so a divergence isolates three ways: an event, a source problem, or a tamper signal.
- **Interoperation with the deployed object world**, with the direction of authority inverted: import their geometry, never their truth, and keep the ledger of how well it tracks.
- **Composition is adjudication**, which promotes the scene grammar to the external ABI while the observation contract stays the internal one — a role change, not an authority change.

## Boundaries

- No cryptography moves credibility, and a system implying otherwise has misdescribed its foundation.
- Punch-card artifacts for reasoning paths that fed a ruling; rebuild-from-source for everything else.
- A congruence failure routes as a candidate; the frozen trace testifies and never decides.
- Exact execution of a wrong model looks more credible than sloppy execution of a right one, and only an independent reference separates the two — which is absent.
- An export is a projection, never a carrier of authority. Vendor-format churn is a source-capture problem.
- Nothing is exported, no host is integrated, and no external object model is ingested.
