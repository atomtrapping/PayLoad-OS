# Archive

Material kept for provenance rather than for reading. Nothing here is current,
nothing here is linked from the live documentation as a description of the
system, and nothing here should be cited as one.

## `architecture-map/`

An Obsidian canvas of every part and its flows, built 2026-09-06 for
brainstorming and last revised at `a728d3d` (2026-09-08). One JSON Canvas of 76
nodes and 76 labelled edges, plus 63 part notes.

It was archived on 2026-09-11 for two reasons that both have to be true
together. First, it had stopped being maintained — 64 commits landed after its
last revision, and its most prominent card still said the admission authority
did not exist while `src/domain/admission.ts` had been 405 lines since
2026-09-07. Second, it was never used for the thing it was built for: 63 of 63
notes still carry an untouched `_Brainstorm here._`, and none of its 115 open
questions was ever ticked.

Its per-part prose duplicated `docs/` by design — 44 of the notes cite the
`docs/` file they restate. The one thing in it written down nowhere else was
that backlog of 115 undecided questions, and those are now in
[`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), which is live.
